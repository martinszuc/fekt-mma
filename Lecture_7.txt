CORS headers

What are CORS headers?
CORS (Cross-Origin Resource Sharing) allows or restricts requests from different origin
domains when accessing Django API.
Default browser policy is to restrict fetching data from different local hosts (ie,
http://localhost:3000 and http://localhost:8000).
Occurs typically, when your front-end application running on one port and the back-end on
the other port.
The reason of restrictions is CORS settings help prevent unauthorized domains from
accessing your API.
